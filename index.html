English Lesson Studio

A personal lesson-production tool for the Sebastiaan Teaches YouTube channel.

The tool runs on the channel owner's Mac. It creates English lesson drafts, teaching images, narration and subtitles, then uploads reviewed videos and organises them in CEFR-aligned A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 and C2 playlists.

Google authorization is used to connect the owner's YouTube channel. This website does not collect credentials, offer public sign-in, or run the lesson workflow.
